# Maintaining entry scholarship and financial aid

This cleaned extract applies to full-time undergraduate VinUniversity students who receive merit-based scholarships or financial aid.

## Scope

Entry merit-based scholarships and need-based financial aid are awarded for the duration of study. Merit-based scholarships are not eligible for scholarship-level upgrades. Students may apply for additional support from other sources under the criteria of each support type.

## Renewal criteria for full and 100% merit scholarships

Renewal requires a cumulative GPA of at least 3.2 in the academic year under evaluation, calculated as the average of Fall and Spring. The student must maintain good disciplinary standing and must not commit Tier 3 major misconduct or Tier 4 extremely serious misconduct. The student must complete the E.X.C.E.L self-evaluation and meet the Advisor to discuss it.

If the academic-year GPA is from 0.0 to 2.49, the scholarship is automatically downgraded by one level under the scholarship scheme for that academic year. If the GPA is from 2.50 to 3.19, students with Full and 100% scholarships may retain the scholarship conditionally and receive one additional regular semester to improve GPA and E.X.C.E.L performance. Special cases may be reviewed by the Committee.
