# Student financial regulations and tariffs

This cleaned extract records selected financial rules for VinUniversity students.

## Tuition and credit-based charges

Listed tuition is issued according to the standard curriculum framework announced at admission. It includes training-practical fees, facilities fees, IT support, and listed student-support activities. Credit-based tuition applies to additional credits for concurrent programs, credits beyond the standard curriculum, selected exchange or visiting-study situations, and course retakes. A retake may be required after failing the minimum passing grade, being ineligible for a final examination because of absence or a breach of academic regulations, or voluntarily seeking academic improvement.

## Library-related fees

Students who have not paid a fine for overdue borrowed materials or equipment cannot continue borrowing library materials or equipment. A student who returns books more than 30 days late must, in addition to late fees, pay a fine equivalent to the cover price of the borrowed document.

Repairing slightly damaged borrowed material or equipment costs 200,000 VND per document or item. For lost or badly damaged materials available for re-order, the fee is the price of a new copy plus a 200,000 VND administrative fee per document. For materials unavailable on the market, the fee is five times the original price. Fees for a lost or badly damaged equipment item are the cost of a new equivalent item plus a 200,000 VND administrative fee.

## Selected academic administrative fees

Student ID card replacement costs 200,000 VND per card. Certified documents cost 50,000 VND per copy. Processing tuition deferment or refund, program transfer, or concurrent enrollment in two programs costs 2,000,000 VND per request. Exam-score review costs 1,000,000 VND per request per subject.
