# Facility usage regulations

This cleaned extract covers library spaces and selected shared facilities at VinUniversity.

## Library spaces

Students, faculty, and staff need a validated University card to access the physical library and borrow physical materials or equipment. A validated VinUniversity ID is required for subscribed electronic materials. Library hours are set by the University and may change; updates are posted at the entrance and on the library website.

Users must maintain a study-friendly environment, avoid disruptive noise, care for library property, and not leave personal belongings unattended. Food, stimulants, weapons, flammable or explosive substances are prohibited. Drinks must be in spill-proof containers, and smoking or vaping is prohibited.

For functional rooms such as group discussion rooms, computer rooms, and studios, reservations should be made in advance. Users must report failures, leave rooms neat and tidy, turn off lights and air conditioning, and return borrowed equipment.

## Parking lot

Vehicles must be parked only in designated marked areas. The maximum speed in the parking lot is 5 km/h. Fire, eating, drinking, and smoking are prohibited there. Vehicle owners remain responsible for personal belongings left in their vehicles.
